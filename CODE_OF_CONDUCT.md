# Code of conduct

Yakumo welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels and language skills. Everyone participating in the project is expected to help keep discussions respectful and useful.

## Scope

This code applies to this repository's issues, pull requests and GitHub Discussions. It applies to contributors and maintainers alike.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and be patient with newcomers.
- Give constructive feedback and explain technical disagreements with evidence.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking the people behind them.
- Respect other people's boundaries and privacy.
- Keep discussions relevant and allow others to participate.

Disagreement with a maintainer, reporting a bug or questioning a technical decision is not a violation of this code.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly targeting or provoking a participant, or continuing unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Spam or deliberate disruption of discussions.

## Maintainer responsibilities

The project maintainers are responsible for applying this code; there is no separate moderation team. They may edit or remove messages, close or lock discussions, and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Maintainers should consider the severity, context and recurrence of a violation, explain their decisions when appropriate, and apply the same standards to everyone. A warning may be enough for a minor incident; serious threats or harassment may require immediate restrictions. There is no mandatory sequence of penalties or guaranteed response time.

A maintainer directly involved in a dispute should leave the decision to another maintainer when one is available.

## GitHub reports

Content that violates GitHub's own rules can also be reported through GitHub's reporting tools. Avoid posting personal information or sensitive details in public discussions about an incident.

For repository contribution rules, see [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). For security vulnerability reports, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
